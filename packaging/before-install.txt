Welcome to Vyoma Handless

Handless lets you control your Windows PC with your voice. Here is exactly what it will use, so there are no surprises:

  MICROPHONE
  To hear your commands and dictation. Audio is captured only while you hold a shortcut (Ctrl + Space, or Ctrl + Shift to dictate). After installing, the setup screen checks the Windows microphone privacy switch for you and runs a quick test.

  SCREEN & UI AUTOMATION
  To find buttons and text fields when you ask it to click or type. Handless uses the built-in Windows UI Automation service. Nothing is recorded in the background.

  WINDOWS CREDENTIAL MANAGER
  Your optional Groq and Gemini API keys are stored here, encrypted by Windows. They are never written to a plain file.

  START WITH WINDOWS (optional)
  If you choose it, Handless starts quietly as a small floating button when you sign in.

No administrator rights are needed. Handless installs for your Windows account only, and you can uninstall it any time from Settings > Apps.
